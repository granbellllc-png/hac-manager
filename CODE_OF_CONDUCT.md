# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors regardless of background, experience or identity. Treat others with respect, assume good intent and keep feedback focused on the work.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical discussion, respect for differing viewpoints, accepting corrections, and protecting others' privacy. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, doxxing and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement should be proportionate and protect the privacy of those involved.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports, ask for relevant context and communicate the outcome privately where possible. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
